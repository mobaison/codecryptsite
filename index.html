I don't follow the famous or chase the crowd.
The quiet one who walks — raahi — logs each road aloud.
Not on a page, but where the square frames the slow.
The.raahi.log — follow the footprint, not the glow.
No destination glitter, just the dirt and stone.
My grid’s a journal, not a throne.

The scroll is split in three — none will surrender easily.
The first speaks the same thing twice —
find it where eyes don't linger.
The second let Julius himself breathe on it —
not by three as he preferred, but by
2 digit number look these two digit
can join together to get a heart —
then it cloaked itself in the language of machines.
Three comments whisper the same shape —
only one has not crossed the Rubicon with a lie.
The third was torn at birth —
its first half sleeps in one memory,
its second in another —
scroll forward to find what you missed going back.

The day you found is not a memory — it is a verdict.
It was not celebrated in the streets.
It was celebrated in the silence before the streets.
What was returned that day cannot be touched, only stood beneath.
Do not search for the event — search for what the event ended.
The place is not named after what it is —
it is named after what it refuses to be.
Face the direction the shadow falls at the first hour —
your threshold is where the stone remembers the soil.
